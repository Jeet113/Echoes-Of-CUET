About Echoes Of CUET

Echoes Of CUET is a community-driven platform that preserves the stories, experiences, and moments that make Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology special.

Every marker on our map represents a real memory shared by a real student or alumnus. From late-night library sessions to victory celebrations at the cricket field, these stories capture the essence of campus life.
